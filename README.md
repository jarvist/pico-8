# Frost Pico-8 Games

Back in December 2022, I bought a copy of
[Pico-8](https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php) : an 8-bit fantasy console
running Lua and with a nice integrated development environment (sprite editor).

I've slowly slowly been writing games with my children since then: generally
they make the art and have a very clear direction of what they want me to code. 
Since 2025, Google Gemini has generally taken over writing the core game
mechanic, and then debugging and extension by me, and the sprites and gameplay
mechanics by the kids.

## Games

### [Submarine Racer](2025-11-submarineracer.html) 2022-2025

You are the submarine. The little sibling submarine will follow you about. 
The Mothership submarine bumbles back and forwards. The crab robot crawls on
the seabed, and fires a seeking missile at you. 

### [Unicorn Jump](unicorn_jump.html) Dec 2025

A game where you play as a unicorn jumping over obstacles on a starry night.
Single control, just jump. 
Written for the four year old, the mechanic is very simple, and holding down
the button actually works quite well. 

### [Asteroid Dodger](asteroid-dodger.html) Dec 2025

You control a spaceship flying through space. Dodge procedurally generated
asteroids while they spawn faster and faster! Try to survive as long as you can
and get a high score. Written for the seven year old. 

### [Melcombe Sheep Hunt](sheep_hunt.html) Aug 2026

Play as Isla, the brown and orange border collie, on a mission to herd runaway
sheep back to Berni's shed on Melcombe. Explore the fields, avoid the hedges
and the sea, and watch the sheep form a lovely conga line behind you! A gentle,
frustration-free exploration game, flip-screen navigation, level progression,
and no 'Game Over' states.
